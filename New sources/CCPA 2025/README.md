# CCPA 2025 — knowledge-graph CSVs

## Source

**California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018**, Civil Code §§ 1798.100 – 1798.199.100, as posted by
the California Privacy Protection Agency in January 2025. The cover page of the source reads:

> CALIFORNIA CONSUMER PRIVACY ACT OF 2018
> effective 01/01/2025 – SB 1223, AB 1008, AB 1824 update
> posted to cppa.ca.gov January 2025

Source PDF: `KB 2/USA/CCPA.pdf` (extracted with `pdftotext -layout`, 65 pages).
Every cell of text in these CSVs is verbatim from that extract; column values that the document
does not state are left empty.

## Relationship to the existing `CPRA` folder

This repository already contains a `CPRA` folder built from the November 2020 Proposition 24
(California Privacy Rights Act) text. **That folder and this one are the same law**, not two
different instruments: Prop 24 amended the CCPA in place, so both cover the same Civil Code
sections (§§ 1798.100 onward) of Title 1.81.5. This folder is simply a **newer snapshot** of that
statute — the text as it stands effective 1 January 2025 — and it is modelled at clause level
across all 45 sections, where the `CPRA` folder covers 16 sections at a coarser grain.
Use this folder for current text; keep `CPRA` only if the 2020 snapshot is needed for comparison.

### What the 2025 snapshot visibly carries

The source names three 2025 measures on its cover page but **does not annotate any section with
which bill amended it**, so no amendment provenance is asserted in these CSVs. What can be stated
from the text itself is that the following clauses are present in this snapshot and carry the
subject matter the cover page names:

| Cover-page measure | Clause present in this text | What the clause says (verbatim, abridged) |
|---|---|---|
| SB 1223 (neural data) | `1798.140(ae)(1)(G)(i)` and `(ii)` | sensitive personal information includes "A consumer's neural data"; "'Neural data' means information that is generated by measuring the activity of a consumer's central or peripheral nervous system, and that is not inferred from nonneural information." |
| AB 1008 (AI / personal information) | `1798.140(v)(4)(A)–(C)` | "'Personal information' can exist in various formats", including "Abstract digital formats, including compressed or encrypted files, metadata, or artificial intelligence systems that are capable of outputting personal information." |
| AB 1824 (merger successor obligations) | `1798.120(a)(2)` | "A business to which another business transfers the personal information of a consumer as an asset that is part of a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, or other transaction … shall comply with a consumer's direction to the transferor made pursuant to this subdivision." |

The mapping of bill → clause above is an inference from subject matter; the document itself only
prints the three bill numbers on its cover page. Nothing else in these CSVs marks amendment status.

## Schema

The statute's own vocabulary is used. California statutory drafting nests
**subdivision (a) → paragraph (1) → subparagraph (A) → clause (i) → subclause (I)**, and those five
names are the `level_type` values on `nodes_Clause.csv`.

### Node types

| File | Type | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `nodes_Section.csv` | Section | One row per Civil Code section, §1798.100 to §1798.199.100 (45). Carries the printed heading (where the statute prints one), the citation, and any text that sits before the first subdivision (`intro_text`). |
| `nodes_Clause.csv` | Clause | One row per lettered/numbered subdivision at any of the five depths — the clause-level extraction. `text` is that clause's **own** verbatim text only (a container clause whose whole body is its children therefore has an empty `text`; 32 rows are such containers). `parent_clause_id` gives the tree. `modals` records which of *shall / shall not / may / may not* the clause text uses. |
| `nodes_Definition.csv` | Definition | Every clause that defines a term with "means" / "has the same meaning". Covers all of §1798.140 plus the local definitions in §§1798.145, 1798.146 and 1798.148. `definition_text` is the full clause text. |
| `nodes_Right.csv` | Right | The seven consumer rights the statute names in its own section headings (delete, correct, know/access, know what is sold or shared, opt out of sale or sharing, limit use of sensitive personal information, no retaliation). |
| `nodes_EnforcementBody.csv` | EnforcementBody | The two bodies the text names by name: the California Privacy Protection Agency and the Attorney General. |
| `nodes_MonetaryAmount.csv` | MonetaryAmount | Distinct dollar figures printed in the statute (revenue threshold, statutory damages, administrative fines, civil penalties, grant floor, appropriations, board per-diem). |
| `nodes_TimePeriod.csv` | TimePeriod | Distinct durations and fixed dates the statute states (45 days, 12 months, five-year, January 1, 2023, …). `period_kind` is `duration` or `fixed date`. |
| `nodes_Threshold.csv` | Threshold | Non-monetary quantitative thresholds (100,000 or more consumers or households; 50 percent; at least a 40 percent interest; the fund allocation percentages; the 250-word addendum limit). |
| `nodes_ExternalCitation.csv` | ExternalCitation | Civil Code sections cited by this title that lie outside it (§1798.80, §1798.81.5). |

## Caveats and gaps

- **No Part/Chapter/Article layer.** The posted text prints only a contents list of sections; it
  does not group them into articles or divisions, so no grouping node type was invented.
- **Section headings.** 20 sections print a heading; 25 (§1798.146, §1798.148, §1798.194,
  §1798.196, §1798.198, §1798.199 and all of §1798.199.10 – §1798.199.100) print only the section
  number in this document, so their `heading` is empty rather than guessed.
- **Container clauses.** 32 clauses exist purely to introduce their children and have empty `text`;
  their content is in their descendants.
- **Monetary amounts are as printed, not as adjusted.** Several amounts in the source carry an
  asterisk and the footnote "Pursuant to Civil Code § 1798.199.95(d), this monetary amount has been
  updated. Please see updated amount." The updated figures are **not** printed in this document, so
  only the statutory figures appear here. The footnote lines themselves were removed as page
  furniture; the CPI-adjustment mechanism is captured in the clause text of §1798.199.95(d).
- **Cross-references are section-level.** A citation such as "paragraph (4) of subdivision (c) of
  Section 1798.135" produces an edge to the section node; the pinpoint wording is preserved in the
  `citation_text` column rather than resolved to a clause node.
- **External law citations.** Only cited Civil Code sections outside this title were made nodes.
  Citations to federal law, the Government Code, the Penal Code, the Education Code, etc. remain
  inside the clause text and were not turned into nodes.
- **Inoperative provisions retained.** §1798.145(m)(4) and (n)(3) state they "shall become
  inoperative on January 1, 2023"; they are still printed in this January 2025 posting and are
  therefore retained as clauses.
- Page headers/footers ("Page N of 65"), the contents list, and the asterisked footnotes were
  removed; all other wording is preserved, with PDF line wrapping rejoined into single-line prose.
