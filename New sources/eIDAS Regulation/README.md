# eIDAS Regulation — knowledge-graph extraction

## Source identification (checked first, as instructed)

Source PDF: `KB 2/Europe/eIDAS Regulation (Electronic Identification and Trust Services).pdf`

The document **is the instrument itself**, not commentary and not a secondary text. It is the
official Official Journal publication of:

> **REGULATION (EU) No 910/2014 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL of 23 July 2014
> on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal
> market and repealing Directive 1999/93/EC** — OJ L 257, 28.8.2014, p. 73.

Specifically it is the **original 2014 text ("eIDAS 1")**, *not*:
- Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 (the "eIDAS 2" amendment establishing the European Digital Identity
  Wallet), and
- not a consolidated version incorporating later amendments.

Evidence: the header block reads `28.8.2014 EN Official Journal of the European Union L 257/73`;
the enacting terms run Article 1 to Article 52 with Annexes I–IV only; there is **no** Chapter II
Section 1 on the European Digital Identity Wallet, no Article 5a/5b/5c, no Annexes V–VII, and no
"electronic attestation of attributes" definitions — all of which are introduced by 2024/1183.
Article 52 states the Regulation applies from 1 July 2016. Everything modelled here is therefore
the 2014 text as enacted.

## Schema

Bespoke to the document's own structure and vocabulary (Chapter / Section / Article / paragraph /
point / Annex / recital).

### Node types
| File | Type | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `nodes_Regulation.csv` | Regulation | The instrument itself (title, OJ reference, legal basis, repealed act) |
| `nodes_Recital.csv` | Recital | The 77 numbered recitals of the preamble, verbatim |
| `nodes_Chapter.csv` | Chapter | Chapters I–VI with their headings |
| `nodes_Section.csv` | Section | The 8 Sections of Chapter III |
| `nodes_Article.csv` | Article | Articles 1–52, with heading, chapeau and full verbatim text |
| `nodes_Provision.csv` | Provision | **Clause-level unit**: every numbered paragraph, unnumbered subparagraph, lettered/roman point and indent inside an Article, verbatim, with `parent_provision_id` |
| `nodes_Definition.csv` | Definition | The 41 defined terms of Article 3, term split from definition text |
| `nodes_AssuranceLevel.csv` | AssuranceLevel | low / substantial / high, with the verbatim criteria from Article 8(2)(a)–(c) |
| `nodes_Annex.csv` | Annex | Annexes I–IV with headings and full text |
| `nodes_AnnexRequirement.csv` | AnnexRequirement | Clause-level requirements inside each Annex, with parent links |

`Provision` uses the document's own labelling: `provision_kind` is one of `paragraph`,
`subparagraph`, `chapeau`, `point`, `indent`. Ids are readable and hierarchical, e.g.
`ART-24.2.f.ii` = Article 24(2)(f)(ii); `ANNEX-II.1.c` = Annex II point 1(c). Unnumbered
subparagraphs (which the Regulation itself cross-refers to as "the first subparagraph") get
synthetic labels `sp1`, `sp2`, … in document order; their text is verbatim.

Trust-service requirements are not a separate node type — the Regulation places them in ordinary
Articles (Chapter III, Sections 1–8: Arts 13–45) and in Annexes I–IV, so they are captured at
clause level as `Provision` / `AnnexRequirement` rows under those Articles and Annexes.

## Method

Generated by script from a `pdftotext -layout` extract. Page headers/footers
(`28.8.2014 EN Official Journal of the European Union L 257/nn`) and the OJ footnote blocks that
precede them were removed; column-wrapped lines were rejoined and soft hyphens repaired, with
wording preserved exactly. No text was paraphrased, summarised or supplied from outside the PDF.

## Caveats and gaps

- **Footnote text is deliberately excluded.** The OJ footnotes (full citations of referenced
  Directives/Regulations, OJ page references) are apparatus, not enacting text. Their in-text
  superscript markers survive as bare `(1)`, `(2)` etc. inside a handful of recital and Article
  strings — this is faithful to the layout extract but is a small typographic artefact.
- `entry_into_force` on the Regulation node is left empty: the document states the rule
  (Article 52(1), "the twentieth day following that of its publication") rather than a date. The
  applicable date of 1 July 2016 is stated in Article 52(2) and is captured verbatim there.
- Cross-reference relationships are derived from literal `Article N` / `Annex N` mentions in
  clause text. References with paragraph precision (e.g. "Article 48(2)") resolve to the Article
  node; references to other instruments (Directive 95/46/EC, Regulation (EU) No 182/2011, etc.)
  are present in the verbatim text but are not modelled as nodes.
- Article 3 definitions appear twice by design: as `Definition` nodes (term/definition split) and
  as `Provision` rows under Article 3 (verbatim clause text).
- Signature block, the "HAVE ADOPTED THIS REGULATION" formula and the "Having regard to…"
  citations are not modelled as nodes; the legal basis citation is stored on the Regulation node.
- Validation run: every node id unique within its file, every relationship endpoint resolves to a
  node id, every node file carries `source_document`. Result: **PASS**.
